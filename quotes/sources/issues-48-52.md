## #48 — Hail, California! — 22:28, ID 2228-001

Mary E. Oliver's book is catalogued by [UC Berkeley](https://digicoll.lib.berkeley.edu/record/278885). The quotation appears on printed page 63 of the [digitized book](https://archive.org/details/hailcalifornia00oliv); the [searchable transcription](https://archive.org/download/hailcalifornia00oliv/hailcalifornia00oliv_djvu.txt) preserves the passage across line breaks.

The surrounding pages establish that this is **22:28**, not the issue's 10:28: page 62 mentions the residence closing at ten-thirty on weeknights, and page 66 explicitly mentions 10:21 p.m. and lights out at eleven. The existing 22:28 quote keeps ID 2228-000; this addition is 2228-001. The title typo “Califorina” is corrected to “California”. No published translation was confirmed in the five non-English locales.
