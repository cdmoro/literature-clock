# Third-party material and quote provenance

## Sources and acknowledgements

Literature Clock builds on [Johannes Enevoldsen's Literature Clock](https://github.com/JohsEnevoldsen/literature-clock) and [Jaap Meijers' literary e-reader clock](https://www.instructables.com/Literary-Clock-Made-From-E-reader/).

The English quote collection used here was taken from the earlier Literature Clock project. Its origins trace back to [The Guardian's reader-contributed literary clock list](https://www.theguardian.com/books/table/2011/apr/21/literary-clock). Further quotations were added over time in the subsequent projects and through contributions. The collection is therefore not an unchanged copy of the original Guardian list. Other language catalogues in this repository include translations and adaptations of the English collection.

Credit is due to those projects, The Guardian and its contributing readers, subsequent contributors, and the authors and works identified alongside the quotations.
